KPI definitions

Churn rate
Churned customers ÷ all customers in the current filter.
Retention rate
1 − churn rate (customers still active ÷ all customers).
Revenue at risk
Sum of MonthlyCharges for churned customers (monthly recurring revenue lost), also shown as % of total monthly revenue.
Tenure KPI
Average TenureMonths of all customers, with churned vs retained shown separately.
Tenure bands
0–6, 7–12, 13–24, 25+ months.

Caveat: with only 15 customers, each customer moves a percentage by about 7 points, so read patterns as directional, not conclusive. Orange vs blue is a colorblind-safe pair, and every bar also carries a text label.
